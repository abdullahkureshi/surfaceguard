I built my first SaaS product: SurfaceGuard 🛡️

Most small businesses never look at their company the way an attacker does. Expired SSL certificates, missing email-spoofing protection, forgotten subdomains, exposed .env files, databases open to the internet. Attackers find these in minutes. The business owner usually never does.

So I built SurfaceGuard, an attack-surface monitoring tool for small businesses and the IT providers (MSPs) who look after them.

What it does:
🔍 A free domain check: get an A–F security grade in about 20 seconds, no signup
✉️ SPF, DMARC and DKIM checks, so attackers can't easily send email as your company
🔒 SSL/TLS, HTTPS and security-header analysis
🌐 Subdomain discovery from public certificate logs, including subdomain-takeover detection
📂 Exposed-file and risky-open-port checks, which only run after the owner proves they own the domain
🔔 Automatic rescans, with an alert when something new appears
📄 A printable client report for MSPs

Things I learned building it:
• A scanner must never make a site look safer just because a check timed out. Honesty in results matters more than a nice score.
• Ethics have to be part of the product's design. Active probing is gated behind DNS ownership verification, and scans of internal IPs are blocked.
• A security product should pass its own checks. SurfaceGuard has a strict security policy and full security headers.

Tech: Python, FastAPI, SQLite, Stripe subscriptions, Docker, and EU hosting (Frankfurt) for GDPR.
